# Inviting Team members

Each project in <SiteName/> has its own dedicated team. You can invite collaborators to your project by sending them an email invitation, assigning them a role that controls their access permissions.

## How to Invite a Team Member

### 1. Navigate to the Team Section

In the dashboard sidebar, open the **Team** tab and click **Members**.

### 2. Click "Invite a Member"

Press the **Invite a Member** button at the top right of the members list.

### 3. Fill in the Invitation Form

You will be asked to provide:

-   **First Name** of the member
-   **Last Name** of the member
-   **Email Address** where the invitation will be sent
-   **Project** (automatically selected based on your current project)
-   **Role to assign** (e.g., Viewer, Developer, Admin, or a custom role)

### 4. Send the Invitation

Click **Send an Invite**. An email invitation will be sent to the specified email address, allowing the user to join your project.
