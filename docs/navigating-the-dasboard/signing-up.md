Before you can start exploring the <SiteName/> Dashboard and deploying AI agents, you'll need to create a <SiteName/> account. Signing up is quick and straightforward, designed to get you up and running with minimal friction.

## How to Sign Up

### 1. Visit the <SiteName/> Dashboard

Go to the <DashboardURL/>.

### 2. Sign Up with Google

Click the **Sign Up** button and choose to authenticate using your Google account.

:::note
Currently, only Google login is supported. Make sure to review and agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before proceeding.
:::

### 3. Complete your profile

After authenticating with Google, you'll be prompted to provide:

-   **First Name**
-   **Last Name**
-   **Bio and Notes** (short description about you and additional information if needed)

You can also optionally upload a profile picture.

### 4. Create your first project

You will then be guided to create your first project.
This project will serve as the starting point for your work within <SiteName/> and will be associated with your account.
To set it up, simply provide:

-   **Project Name**
-   **Project Description**

### 5. Finish Setup

Click **Finish** to complete the signup process. You will then be taken directly to your <SiteName/> dashboard, ready to begin building with your new project.
