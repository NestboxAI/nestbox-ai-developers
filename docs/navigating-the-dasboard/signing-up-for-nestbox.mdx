# Signing up for Nestbox

Before you can start exploring the Nestbox Dashboard and deploying AI agents, you'll need to create a Nestbox account. Signing up is quick and straightforward, designed to get you up and running with minimal friction.

## How to Sign Up

### 1. Visit the Nestbox Dashboard 

Go to the [**Nestbox Dashboard**](https://demo.nestbox.ai/).

### 2. Sign Up with Google

Click the **Sign Up** button and choose to authenticate using your Google account.

:::note
Currently, only Google login is supported. Make sure to review and agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before proceeding.
:::

### 3. Complete your profile

After authenticating with Google, you'll be prompted to provide:

- **First Name**
- **Last Name**
- **Bio and Notes** (short description about you and additional information if needed)


You can also optionally upload a profile picture.

### 4. Create your first project

You will then be guided to create your first project.
This project will serve as the starting point for your work within Nestbox and will be associated with your account.
To set it up, simply provide:
- **Project Name**
- **Project Description**

### 5. Finish Setup

Click **Finish** to complete the signup process. You will then be taken directly to your Nestbox dashboard, ready to begin building with your new project.
