# Welcome to the Nestbox AI Developer Portal

## Introduction to Nestbox

Nestbox delivers specialized, self-hosted AI solutions designed to help mid-market companies unlock new levels of productivity, efficiency, and decision-making.
Our platform empowers organizations to build intelligent agents, automate complex workflows, and enhance operations — all while maintaining full control over their data and infrastructure.

At Nestbox, we believe AI should adapt to your business, not the other way around.
Our flexible, secure solutions seamlessly integrate into your existing systems, giving you the tools to drive real-world results without compromising on customization, scalability, or compliance.

Whether you're looking to optimize internal processes, improve customer experiences, or create entirely new AI-driven services, Nestbox provides the foundation for building smarter, faster, and more resilient businesses.

## The Nestbox Developer Portal

The Nestbox Developer Portal is your comprehensive guide to unlocking the full potential of Nestbox’s AI solutions.
It is designed for developers, engineers, and technical teams who want to integrate, customize, and scale AI agents and workflows with precision and confidence.

Within the portal, you’ll find:

-   Step-by-step onboarding guides to help you get started quickly.
-   In-depth references for APIs, SDKs, and the Nestbox platform functionalities.
-   Hands-on tutorials, robust examples, and troubleshooting advice.
-   Best practices for secure, scalable, and efficient integrations.
-   Opportunities for community engagement, feedback, and contributions.

Whether you're building a new AI-driven workflow, automating key business processes, or fine-tuning existing agents, the Nestbox Developer Portal provides the resources you need to succeed at every stage of development.

## Documentation Structure

The Nestbox Developer Portal is organized into several major sections to support your development journey:

### Navigating the Dashboard

This section introduces the Nestbox web dashboard and its core functionalities. You’ll learn how to navigate the dashboard, manage AI agents and datasets, and configure integrations. It also defines the key terminology used throughout the Nestbox platform, helping you develop a clear and consistent understanding of the system.

### Using the Nestbox CLI

For developers who prefer working from the command line, the Nestbox CLI provides full access to the platform’s capabilities.
This section covers installation, authentication, and usage of the CLI, enabling you to manage resources, automate tasks, and perform operations programmatically — mirroring the capabilities of the dashboard.

### Working with Nestbox APIs

Nestbox exposes a set of powerful APIs to give developers fine-grained control over the platform’s functionalities.
This section is divided into detailed guides for each API:

-   **Admin API**: Manage platform-wide settings, authentication, and user roles.
-   **Documents API**: Store, retrieve, and manage documents used in AI training and operations.
-   **Instances API**: Deploy, monitor, and control the operational instances of AI agents and data processors.
-   **Agents API**: Create, configure, and manage specialized AI agents tailored to your business workflows.

Each API guide provides comprehensive reference materials, example requests and responses, and best practices to ensure secure and efficient integration.
